What this will not tell you

Every other app puts a number here. We know what those numbers are worth, so we are not going to show you one.

Recovery score: 72
There is no such measurement. It is a weighted guess presented as a fact, and the weights are chosen to feel plausible, not to be true.
Stress: elevated
HRV moves with training, alcohol, illness, caffeine and simply standing up, as readily as with stress. One reading cannot separate them.
Sleep stage: deep
Ours is 50 % accurate on real scored nights — barely better than a coin flip, and worse than guessing on 10 of 16 people we tested. Until motion data fixes that, you get no stage.

What one heart rate sensor actually reaches

Health has five parts. This connection lights up two and a half of them. The dark ones are not coming soon — they are answered by asking you.

Beat intervals are read from Bluetooth characteristic 0x2A37 at 1/1024 s resolution and never leave this page. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.
