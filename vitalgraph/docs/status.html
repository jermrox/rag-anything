Vybe Health · capability report

What this system can measure, and what it only guesses at

A health engine is only worth what its weakest claim is worth. This page lists every sensor reading we can decode, every factor we cannot sense, and the one accuracy number we have actually measured against expert-scored human sleep — including the ten subjects it fails on.

01 · Sensor layer

Readings we can decode off a device

Bluetooth health characteristics with a working decoder and tests behind them. Absent measurements stay absent: an IEEE 11073 “not a number” comes back as nothing, never as a zero that a chart would draw.

UUIDCharacteristicYieldsWhat it is not
0x2A37 Heart Rate Measurement Beats per minute, plus RR intervals at 1/1024 s resolution RR intervals are the whole basis for HRV. Most bands never expose them.
0x2A9C Body Composition Fat percentage, muscle mass, water, basal metabolism Impedance estimates, not a scan. Trends are worth more than any one reading.
0x2A9D Weight Measurement Body mass, in kg at 5 g or lb at 10 mg resolution Resolution differs by unit; the decoder keeps them apart.
0x2A18 Glucose Measurement Concentration, sample type, sample location, sequence number Meter data. Nothing on a wrist measures this today.
0x2A35 Blood Pressure Systolic, diastolic, mean arterial pressure, pulse rate Carries status flags: body movement, loose cuff, irregular pulse.
0x2A53 Running Speed and Cadence Instantaneous speed, cadence, stride length, total distance Foot pod or shoe sensor. A wrist infers cadence; this measures it.
0x2A5B Cycling Speed and Cadence Wheel and crank revolutions with event timestamps Counters roll over; the decoder reports raw counts, not derived speed.
02 · The five factors

Three of these five cannot be sensed from a wrist

Health is organised into five factors, and the honest answer to “can a band see this?” is completely different for each. Connection has no passive input at all, deliberately: six meetings in a calendar is not a social-cohesion score, and turning one into the other is false precision.

Sleep

Passively sensed

Am I restoring?

Sensed without asking
sleep_duration, sleep_timing, sleep_consistency, hrv, resting_heart_rate, respiratory_rate, skin_temperature, circadian_phase
Requires the person
psqi, subjective_sleep_quality, fatigue_rating, travel_and_jet_lag
Characteristics decoded
0x2A1C, 0x2A37, 0x2A5F

The strongest factor for wrist sensing. Duration, timing and consistency are directly measurable, and HRV, resting heart rate and respiratory rate all follow from RR intervals alone. Staging is the weak part: it is inferred, not measured.

Movement

Passively sensed

Am I physically capable and appropriately loaded?

Sensed without asking
steps, training_load, heart_rate, vo2max_estimate, sedentary_time, workout_detection
Requires the person
rpe, soreness, body_composition_scan, strength_tests, mobility, external_training_apps
Characteristics decoded
0x2A53, 0x2A5B

Well covered passively for volume and intensity. Capability -- strength, mobility, whether load is *appropriate* -- is not sensed at all and needs active input.

Nutrition

Passively sensed

Am I adequately fuelled?

Sensed without asking
body_mass, body_composition, glucose
Requires the person
food_logging, hydration, caffeine, alcohol, supplements, meal_photos
Characteristics decoded
0x2A18, 0x2A9C, 0x2A9D

The weakest factor for wrist sensing: a wrist cannot see food. The passive inputs listed here arrive from paired instruments -- a scale (0x2A9D), a body-composition device (0x2A9C), a glucose sensor (0x2A18) -- not from the band. Claiming otherwise is the clearest place this model could start hand-waving.

Mind

Passively sensed

How am I functioning psychologically and cognitively?

Sensed without asking
hrv, resting_heart_rate, sleep_derived_signals
Requires the person
mood, perceived_stress, cognitive_tests, reaction_time, burnout_survey, journaling
Characteristics decoded
none

Physiological arousal is measurable; psychological state is not. HRV is a stress *proxy* and a poor one at the individual level -- it moves with training load, alcohol, illness and position as readily as with stress. This factor leans on active input, and the passive signals mainly indicate when to ask.

Connection

Only by asking

How connected and supported is my life?

Sensed without asking
nothing — this factor is invisible to a band
Requires the person
loneliness_scale, social_connectedness, relationship_support, time_with_others
Characteristics decoded
none

No passive input, deliberately. Calendar and communication patterns can generate hypotheses -- and do, in the hypothesis engine -- but six meetings is not a social-cohesion score. Scheduled interaction is not connection, and turning one into the other is false precision. This factor is measured by asking.

03 · Medical context

Not a sixth factor — a layer beneath all five

A beta blocker does not belong to a factor. It changes what every heart-rate-derived reading in three of them means. Modelling context as a modifier rather than a peer is what lets an interpretation say why it was adjusted.

ContextKindReframesHow the reading changes
blood pressure vital sign movement, sleep, mind Elevated readings change what a training recommendation may say and make an unexplained resting heart rate rise more significant.
body composition body composition movement, nutrition Distinguishes mass change that is lean tissue from mass change that is not, which is the difference between a training block working and a diet failing.
beta blocker medication sleep, movement, mind Suppresses heart rate and blunts its response to load. Every heart-rate-derived reading -- resting heart rate, training zones, recovery -- must be read against this and never compared to an untreated population baseline.
atrial fibrillation diagnosis sleep, mind Time-domain HRV becomes uninterpretable: RMSSD is elevated by the arrhythmia itself, not by parasympathetic tone. Reporting a recovery score from it would invert the truth.
thyroid function lab result sleep, movement, nutrition, mind Shifts resting heart rate, temperature and energy availability together, so a simultaneous drift across several factors has a single explanation rather than four.
SSRI medication sleep, mind Alters sleep architecture and can reduce HRV independently of stress, so a declining trend is not evidence of worsening psychological state.

05 · The measured result

Sleep staging from heart rate alone: 50.4%

Trained and tested on 16 nights of MIT-BIH polysomnography — 8,622 thirty-second epochs a technician scored by hand. Held out one whole subject at a time, so no night appears on both sides of the split. The same model scores 100% on our own simulator, which is why the simulator number is worthless and this one is not.

Beat the subject’s own baseline Did not — guessing the commonest stage would score as well or better Per-subject detail is from the 12-feature run.

Read this before quoting the average

The worst subject, slp01a, scores 9.2% where predicting one stage for the whole night scores 44.4%. 10 of 16 subjects show no skill at all. A mean of 50.4% across four classes — 4,792 light, 2,753 awake, 548 deep, 529 REM — is not a staging product. It is a measurement of how far heart rate alone gets you, which is not far.

What moved the number, and what did not

Two label sets, same protocol. Temporal context — a 7.5-minute centred rolling average plus a 2-minute trailing one, computed strictly within a night — is the only change that helped, and it helped on both, which is what makes it credible rather than a lucky split. Frequency-domain HRV made things worse and is shipped off.

06 · Not built

The gaps, named

Listed here rather than left out, because a capability report that only lists capabilities is marketing.

Accelerometer input

The single most likely reason staging is stuck. Every wrist-staging method worth copying uses motion alongside heart rate. The dataset we can reach has no accelerometer channel at all.

The domain knowledge graphs

Twenty subject-area graphs are defined with routing and seeded catalogues, and none has been built. Building them needs an LLM key; search over the harvested code does not, and works today.

Nutrition, Mind and Connection input

Three of the five factors are answered by asking, and there is no surface for asking yet. The passive half of the product is much further along than the conversational half.

Anything clinical

Lab ingestion, FHIR observations, evidence-graded recommendations, the audit chain and the mode gate are designed and unbuilt. Nothing here should be pointed at a patient.

Multi-user isolation

One user, one SQLite file. The citation scheme already carries a user segment; the enforcement behind it does not exist.
